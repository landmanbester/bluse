Read the membership figures (cluster ARI, family ARI), not the composite. The composite scores -1 as an ordinary label, so a configuration leaving half its points unclustered is credited for every within-noise pair. Measured: leaf scores 0.480 composite against eom's 0.024 — a 20× apparent advantage — while on membership the two are 0.032 and 0.028. Noise agreement is near-degenerate for eom, which clusters 99.9% of points; record it, do not gate on it.
Family ARI is the one that matters. Cluster ids are batch artefacts — one physical population is minted afresh in every batch and epoch — so membership at cluster level sits at the noise floor under both selection methods. Matching is what should recover it: measured on sband_short, eom goes 0.028 → 0.519 at the p50 cut, an 18.6× gain.
